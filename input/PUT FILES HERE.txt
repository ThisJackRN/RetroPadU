Put two things in this folder, then double-click BUILD.cmd:

1. Your Mario Kart Wii USA disc image (RMCE01)
   - .iso, .wbfs, .wdf, .wia or .ciso
   - .rvz / .nkit will not work: convert it to .iso in Dolphin first
     (right-click the game > Convert File... > ISO)
   - Only the USA version is supported

2. The Retro Rewind pack folder "RetroRewind6"
   - Download the pack from https://rwfc.net/downloads and extract it
   - The folder must contain Binaries\Code.pul
   - It can be here directly (input\RetroRewind6) or inside the folder you extracted

3. Optional: your old save and VR in the "save" folder
   - RRRating.pul (VR), rksys.dat and banner.bin (save)
   - They are imported automatically the first time the game starts
   - See save\PUT YOUR OLD SAVE HERE.txt for where to find them

Example:
   input\Mario Kart Wii (USA).iso
   input\RetroRewind6\Binaries\Code.pul
   input\save\RRRating.pul
